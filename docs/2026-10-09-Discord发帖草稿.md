## 中文版（频道正文语言）

大家好！我是 kompile.xyz 的作者，一名来自中国的大学生，也是 Compile 的玩家。

这是我自己做的**网页版 Compile（译世界）**：https://kompile.xyz/

免费、无广告、无账号，**不用下载**，手机或电脑打开浏览器就能玩，也支持两台设备联机对战。
我问过原作者 Michael Yang，他很友善地同意我在这里发布这个项目。

跟其他玩家做的版本相比，我觉得比较特别的地方：

1. **每个协议都有自己的特效**：所有协议的卡牌在触发各自协议特色效果时都带专属特效，每个协议**编译之后**也都有独一无二的特效。
2. **1~3 代协议全部做完**（45 套），并支持**随机协议池**模式（开局从全部协议里随机抽 12 套）和**自定义协议池**（自己挑至少 12 套）。
3. **免下载、网页直接联机**：两台设备点对点连接（P2P），直连打不通时自动走中继。
4. 其他已经能玩到的：16 关互动式新手教学（含序章）、中英双语界面、图鉴（按 30 类效果筛选）、卡组与档案导出导入 + 对局重放、更新日志；另外移植了 **COMPILER · Card Builder**，可以做自己的卡牌与协议。

有三件事想跟大家说：

- 我是一名中国的大学生，**不能保证这个项目完全没有 bug**，但我会尽可能地去优化大家发来的意见 —— 游戏首页有「反馈」入口，也可以直接回帖告诉我。
- **英文是我完全机翻的**：如果有哪里翻译错了或者漏翻了，请反馈给我，谢谢🙏
- 之后我打算做两件事：① 帮玩家开发**制作优秀、开发成本不大**的 DIY 协议；② 实现对原版协议/卡牌的**加强或修改**想法 —— 这一部分会做成设置里**可关闭的「加强卡牌模式」**（**默认关闭**），关着的时候你体验到的仍然是原卡牌的效果。

**致敬**：原作作者 **Michael Yang**；卡牌制作器 **COMPILER · Card Builder** 的作者 **Albert Blanco**（MIT 许可，本项目已按其许可在制作器页面署名）；以及带我入坑 Compile 的 **NZ3ROK**。

请享受这个项目吧。

## 英文版

Hi everyone! I'm the developer of kompile.xyz — a university student from China and a Compile player.

This is a **browser version of Compile** I built: https://kompile.xyz/

Free, no ads, no account, **no download** — it runs in the browser on phone or desktop, and supports online play between two devices. I asked Michael Yang, the original creator, and he kindly allowed me to share it here.

What makes it different from other fan versions:

1. **Every protocol has its own effects**: each card's protocol signature effect comes with its own animations, and **every protocol has a unique effect when compiled**.
2. **Generations 1-3 are all implemented** (45 protocols), with a **random pool** mode (12 drawn at the start) and a **custom pool** (pick at least 12).
3. **No download, online play in the browser**: two devices connect peer-to-peer, with a relay fallback.
4. Also in: a 16-level interactive tutorial (with a prologue), Chinese/English UI, a library (filter by 30 effect types), deck and archive export/import, match replay, and a ported **COMPILER · Card Builder** for making your own cards and protocols.

Three things to say:

- I'm a student in China and **I can't promise it's bug-free**, but I'll do my best to work through your feedback — there's a Feedback button on the home screen, or just reply here.
- **The English text is fully machine-translated**; if you spot a mistake or something missing, please tell me. Thank you 🙏
- Next up: (1) well-made, low-cost DIY protocols for players, and (2) buffs or modifications to original protocols and cards. That will be an optional **"Enhanced cards" mode in Settings, off by default** — with it off you still get the original effects.

Credits: **Michael Yang** (creator of Compile); **Albert Blanco**, author of **COMPILER · Card Builder** (MIT, credited in-app); **NZ3ROK**, who got me into Compile.

Please enjoy it.
